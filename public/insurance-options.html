When we say don't file

A claim is not always the cheaper option

  • —The repair comes in under your deductible
  • —The loss is small and your renewal is close
  • —The cause is seepage or maintenance, which Hawaii policies exclude
  • —It is mold only, which most policies here cap very low or exclude outright

A claim on record can follow you into renewal, and given what has happened to condo premiums in Hawaii lately that is worth a moment of thought before you phone your carrier.
